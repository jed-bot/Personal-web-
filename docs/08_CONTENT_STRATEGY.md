# Content Strategy

> Content is not filler. Every word must reinforce the [[02_PORTFOLIO_THEME|theme's]] emotional goal.

---

## Voice

| Trait | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| Professional | Clear, confident, no filler |
| Specific | Real tools, real numbers, real outcomes |
| Approachable | Not corporate — human, warm |
| Concise | Short sentences, no fluff |

---

## Messaging Goals

1. Communicate expertise without bragging
2. Show process, not just output
3. Build trust through specificity
4. Guide visitors to contact/hire

---

## Section Copy Guidelines

### Hero

- 1 line: Who you are
- 1 line: What you build
- 1 line: Why it matters

See [[04_UI_GUIDELINES]] for hero layout.

### About

- Focus on capabilities, not biography
- Mention Full Stack, UI/UX, AI specializations
- Keep to 2–3 short paragraphs

### Projects

- Lead with the problem, not the tech
- Show the result before the process
- Use [[07_CASE_STUDY_STRUCTURE]] for narrative flow

### Contact

- Simple, direct CTA
- One clear action: "Let's build something"

---

## Emotional Arc

| Stage | Visitor Thinks | Content Must Do |
|-------|----------------|-----------------|
| Arrival | "Who is this?" | Hero answers immediately |
| Browsing | "This looks professional" | Clean layout, smooth animations |
| Deep dive | "They really know their craft" | Case studies show process |
| Conversion | "I want to work with them" | Clear contact CTA |

See [[02_PORTFOLIO_THEME]] for the full emotional goal.

---

## What to Avoid

- Buzzwords without substance
- Long paragraphs
- Generic descriptions ("passionate developer")
- Jargon the client wouldn't understand
- Listing technologies without context

---

## SEO Considerations

- Every page needs a unique `<title>` and meta description
- Use semantic headings (H1 → H2 → H3)
- Alt text on all images
- See [[09_SEO_GUIDE]] for full SEO checklist

---

## Accessibility in Content

- Write in plain language
- Use proper heading hierarchy
- Link text must be descriptive (never "click here")
- See [[10_ACCESSIBILITY]] for full requirements
