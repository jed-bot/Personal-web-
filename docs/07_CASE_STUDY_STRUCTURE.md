# Case Study Structure

> The project showcase should feel like a **product landing page**, not a list of features.

---

## Philosophy

Each case study tells a story. The visitor should understand the problem, the process, and the result — and feel confident in the quality of work.

See [[02_PORTFOLIO_THEME]] for the overall emotional goal.

---

## Narrative Flow

```
Problem
  ↓
Research
  ↓
Design
  ↓
Development
  ↓
Result
```

### 1. Problem

- What was the client's/user's need?
- What constraints existed?
- Why did this project matter?

### 2. Research

- What did discovery look like?
- What competitors/analyses were reviewed?
- What insights shaped the direction?

### 3. Design

- Wireframes, prototypes, iterations
- Design decisions and rationale
- How [[03_DESIGN_SYSTEM]] was applied

### 4. Development

- Tech stack choices
- Architecture decisions
- Challenges solved

### 5. Result

- Outcomes and impact
- Screenshots of the final product
- Testimonials if available

---

## Visual Requirements

| Element | Spec |
|---------|------|
| Phone mockups | Large, 32px radius, ambient shadow |
| Wide screenshots | Full-width, 24px radius |
| Headings | Large, bold, tight spacing |
| Whitespace | Generous — let the work breathe |

See [[05_COMPONENT_LIBRARY]] for phone mockup and image component specs.

---

## Section Layout

1. **Hero** — Project name, one-liner, hero image
2. **Overview** — Role, timeline, tech stack
3. **Deep Dive** — The 5-step narrative
4. **Gallery** — Full-width screenshots
5. **Results** — Metrics, outcomes
6. **Reflection** — What was learned

---

## Content Voice

- Professional but not cold
- Specific — mention real tools, real problems
- Show thinking, not just output
- See [[08_CONTENT_STRATEGY]] for voice guidelines
